WELCOME TO NEBAN

Education designed to help every child grow.

Neban International Schools is a learning community located in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State. The school provides a structured educational journey from the early years through primary and secondary levels.

With quality teachers, a library, computer laboratory, playground, sick bay and a comfortable learning environment, students are supported academically and personally as they progress through school.
